## Question
What is this map's destination — design locked, working v0, or public launch?

## Resolution
Working v0 (option B). The riskiest fog (how much of civil-platform generalizes, fold/diff
semantics, conformance format) only resolves on contact with real code, so a paper-only
destination under-shoots; launch is a distinct effort with different skills and gets its
own map later. "Done" = the destination statement on the map. The civil-platform
switchover is explicitly out of scope.
