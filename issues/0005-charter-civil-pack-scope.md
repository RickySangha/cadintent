## Question
What does the v0 civil discipline pack contain?

## Resolution
Gravity piped networks, sanitary + storm in one pack: node types manhole, catch basin,
outfall/headwall, generic-structure escape hatch; conduits with size/material/slope/inverts;
network topology. Storm and sanitary are structurally one thing, and one-pack-two-disciplines
demonstrates the shared-core claim. Deferred: pressure networks (fitting-catalog/deflection
legality is its own design effort), alignments/stationing (drags in profile space), surfaces
(TIN/interpolation subproject). Consequence accepted: v0 example elevations are authored
numbers with provenance, no elev_at.
