## Question
Public from day one, or private until v0?

## Resolution
Public from day one under a fresh dedicated GitHub org, unannounced. Rewrite-with-reference
guarantees nothing private ever enters; born-public means no flip-day audit, no tracker
migration, and the planning history itself becomes public archaeology — an asset for a
standards-shaped project.
